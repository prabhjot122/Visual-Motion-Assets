# Static posts and carousels

Use the shared typography, paper/stone materials, window light, ink/clay palette and meaningful geometric lines. Build a composition for the actual format; do not simply freeze or crop a moving scene.

For a static: one clear message, a strong hierarchy, sufficient reading space and a deliberate closing/brand treatment. Tiny UI text does not become readable just because the exported image is high resolution. Use a faithful detail crop or rebuild the required product fragment at a useful size.

For a carousel: plan a hook, a sequence of understandable steps and a useful final action. Each slide must stand on its own while retaining a visual continuation through repeated alignment, an evolving motif or an unfinished line carried into the next page. Do not force the reel's timestamps or one-word captions into slides.

Confirm platform/output dimensions, safe margins, slide count, body copy, accessibility/alt text and source claims in the brief. Deliver the agreed image files plus editable source. Preserve factual context and source attribution when showing documents. Review every slide at normal phone size and in order, not only as a tiled sheet.
