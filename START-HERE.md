## Authority, highest first

1. The current user's instructions and the new project's approved brief/script/voiceover.
2. This file and the shared [brand](brand/visual-language.md), [motion](motion/principles.md) and [sound](sound/music-and-sfx.md) guidance.
3. The relevant [format guide](formats/README.md).
4. Annotated examples: [Reel V3](examples/reel-v3/README.md), [Frame C](examples/frame-c/README.md), [website film](examples/website-film/README.md).
5. Historical source under [archive](archive/README.md), consulted selectively for a mechanism or implementation.

Archive timestamps, machine paths, status words and old 'standing' rules never override a new brief. The historical creative-memory.md is a feedback record for those films, not a current global instruction file. Do not recursively read the archive or all media on startup.

## Agent workflow

1. Create a new folder under projects and record inputs, output specifications, objectives, factual sources and permissions/budget in its brief. See [template](templates/project-brief.md).
2. Inspect the actual media and script. If voiceover exists, measure duration and align the speech; do not invent exact timecodes from text. If it does not exist, label timing provisional and follow the brief's narration-generation authority.
3. Study the annotated successful example, Frame C and the relevant reference passages. Do not infer motion from thumbnails alone.
4. Develop a visual story for this script: hook, information hierarchy, scene objectives, continuity and readable holds. Record choices and uncertain facts. Default to autonomous creation and local delivery within supplied inputs/authority; follow any checkpoints explicitly requested by the brief rather than imposing the old film's per-section approval gates. Publication, paid generation and purchases still require their own authority.
5. Build with editable text/shapes/UI where motion needs them. Reuse materials and suitable source mechanisms; do not merely paste app screenshots and add zooms.
6. Render, inspect representative frames and transitions, verify audio/caption alignment and final media properties, and deliver actual files with reproducible commands and disclosed limitations. Never claim listening or real-time playback unless performed.
7. Keep feedback in that project. Propose durable preferences for [promotion](LEARNING-POLICY.md); never silently rewrite the shared style because one shot changed.

## Not universal

No fixed 62.9/85.4-second runtime, four pauses, influencer split, hearing slot, app-screen starting second, specific source corpus, mandatory lawyer-office opening, one-word-title policy, serif-only website caption policy or 'Three.js only for stacks' rule carries into every project. Those are historical decisions. Tools may change when justified; the visual character and quality expectations are the reusable part.

No new project may portray Arora, Vikram or the Arif Jafar OCR scan as one case, turn historical demo dates into court listings, or treat sample figures as factual evidence for a fresh claim. Product demonstrations need current evidence for current capabilities.
